Delete your SafeAlert account

Request permanent deletion of your account and associated data

You can delete your SafeAlert account and related personal data at any time. Deletion removes your profile and associated account data from our production systems, subject to short technical retention needed for security and legal compliance.

Option 1 — In the app (recommended)

  1. Open SafeAlert and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Choose Delete account (or equivalent) and confirm.

Option 2 — Web request

If you cannot access the app, email us from the phone number linked to your account:

To: celestinkas@gmail.com

Subject: SafeAlert account deletion request

Include: your SafeAlert phone number (E.164, e.g. +243…) and a short confirmation that you want the account deleted.

We aim to process web deletion requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Account profile (phone, pseudo, role settings)
  • Authentication / session data tied to your account
  • Personal settings (e.g. FCM token, presence preferences)

Some safety records may be anonymized or retained in limited form where required for security, abuse prevention, or legal obligations.
